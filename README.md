# India CPI Inflation Analysis | Excel Case Study

## Project Overview

This project explores India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) to understand inflation trends, category-wise contributions, food price movements, the impact of COVID-19, and the relationship between imported crude oil prices and inflation.

The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, with data cleaning, formulas, percentage-change calculations, category mapping, correlation analysis, and interactive visualisations.

## Project Objectives

The project addresses five key analytical questions:

1. **CPI Category Contribution:** Analyse the contribution of broader expenditure categories towards the overall CPI basket.
2. **Annual Inflation Trends:** Examine the year-on-year growth of India's Rural+Urban CPI General Index and identify the year with the highest inflation.
3. **Food Inflation Analysis:** Investigate monthly food inflation trends, identify the highest and lowest inflation months, and analyse changes across food subcategories.
4. **COVID-19 Impact:** Compare inflation before and after March 2020, focusing on Food and Beverages, Health, and Essential Services.
5. **Crude Oil and Inflation:** Explore the relationship between imported Indian Basket crude oil prices and inflation across different CPI categories.

## Dataset

- **Source:** Government of India CPI dataset.
- **Coverage:** 2013 to May 2023.
- **Geographical segments:** Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban.
- **Data frequency:** Monthly.
- **Additional data:** Indian Basket Crude Oil FOB prices.

## Analytical Approach

### 1. Category Mapping and Contribution
Created a mapping sheet to group individual CPI subcategories into broader expenditure buckets and compared their relative shares.

### 2. Annual Inflation Analysis
Examined changes in the General CPI Index over different years and visualised the inflation trend.

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis
Used the Food and Beverages parent index to calculate monthly inflation, identify the highest and lowest inflation months, and compare individual food subcategories.

### 4. COVID-19 Impact Analysis
Used March 2020 as the reference point and compared monthly, three-month, and annual inflation movements across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.

### 5. Crude Oil Correlation Analysis
Aligned monthly crude oil prices with CPI category data and explored correlations between the two datasets.

## Note on Methodology

Some analytical measures in this project use equal-weighted category averages for custom comparisons. These should not be interpreted as official CPI expenditure weights or official contribution estimates. Correlation indicates association and does not establish causation.
